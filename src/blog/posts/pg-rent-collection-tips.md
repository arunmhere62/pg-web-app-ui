# PG Rent Collection Tips: How to Collect Rent on Time Every Month

Late rent payments are the biggest headache for PG owners in India. Chasing tenants every month, maintaining Excel sheets, and sending WhatsApp messages manually — it's exhausting and inefficient.

In this article, we'll share proven rent collection strategies that work, and show you how to automate the entire process.

## Why Tenants Pay Late

Understanding why tenants delay payments helps you address the root cause:

1. **They forget** — no reminder sent
2. **No clear due date** — ambiguous payment timeline
3. **No invoice** — tenant doesn't know the exact amount
4. **No consequence** — late payment has no penalty
5. **Payment friction** — no easy payment method
6. **Personal emergencies** — genuine financial difficulties

## 7 Proven Rent Collection Strategies

### 1. Set Clear Rent Cycle Rules

Choose a rent cycle and stick to it:
- **Calendar cycle**: Rent due on 1st, late from 5th
- **Mid-month cycle**: Rent due on 15th, late from 20th

Communicate this to tenants at the time of onboarding and include it in the rental agreement.

### 2. Send Reminders Before Due Date

Don't wait until rent is overdue. Send reminders:
- **3 days before** due date — friendly reminder
- **On due date** — payment reminder with invoice
- **2 days after** — follow-up with late fee warning
- **5 days after** — final notice

**IPGM automates all of these** via WhatsApp and SMS — you never have to send a manual reminder.

### 3. Use Professional Invoices

A professional invoice builds trust and makes payment feel official. Include:
- Tenant name and room/bed number
- Rent period (e.g., January 2026)
- Rent amount
- Previous balance (if any)
- Payment due date
- Payment methods accepted

IPGM generates professional invoices automatically for every rent cycle.

### 4. Track Partial Payments

Some tenants pay in installments — ₹3,000 now, ₹2,000 next week. Without proper tracking, you'll lose track of who owes what.

**With IPGM:**
- Record partial payments with a single click
- System automatically calculates remaining balance
- Next reminder includes the pending amount
- Full payment history visible per tenant

### 5. Accept Digital Payments

Make it easy for tenants to pay:
- **UPI** — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm
- **Bank transfer** — NEFT/IMPS
- **Cash** — record manually with receipt

Share your UPI ID or QR code in every reminder message. IPGM includes payment details in all WhatsApp reminders.

### 6. Implement Late Fees

A small late fee (₹50-100/day after 5 days) creates urgency. Make sure this is mentioned in the rental agreement.

IPGM can track late fees and add them to the next invoice automatically.
