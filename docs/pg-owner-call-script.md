## Objection Handlers

**"I'll think about it"** → Sir, free setup pani tharen. Try panni paaru — ok illa na stop pannalam. Risk onnum illa sir.

**"I already have an app"** → Super sir. But IPGM-la Google listing free-ah kedaikum — adhu unga business-a increase pannum. Innum oru app try panni paarthu kooda kashtam illa — setup namba side-ah free-ah pani tharen.

**"I don't have time"** → Adhu dhaan advantage sir. Intha app vachi unga time save aagum. Setup-um namba pani tharen — ungalku onnum panna vendam. Just 10 min demo paarthu decide pannunga.

**"My staff handles everything"** → Adhu romba nalla sir. Staff-kum intha app kudutha — avanga work innum easy aagum. Rent tracking, tenant chat, current bill — ellam app-la automatic. Staff-um happy, neengalum happy.

**"How much does it cost?"** → Setup and onboarding completely free sir. Subscription plans nalla affordable-ah irukku. Demo paakku, unga PG-kku enna plan fit-nu decide pannalam.

## Tips

- **Listen more than you talk** — let them describe their pain
- **Match features to their pain** — don't list everything, pick what's relevant
- **Always close with a question** — get their number, schedule next step
- **Follow up next day** — send the link immediately, call as promised
- **Keep it casual** — this is a conversation, not a pitch
